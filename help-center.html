Help Center

Artwork Guidelines

Provide PDF/X with outlined fonts, 3mm bleed, and safety margins. Include dielines on separate spot color layer named “DIELINE”.

Color Management

CMYK (FOGRA39/GRACoL) profiles, Pantone for spot. Request contract proof for critical color. Monitors should be calibrated (D65, 120 cd/m²).

File Delivery

Upload via email (≤20MB) or FTP: ftp.tppc.example. For SFTP, request secure credentials.
